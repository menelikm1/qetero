How Qetero works

From search to site — in three straightforward steps, all inside Telegram.

🔍
1

Browse on Telegram

Open the @qetero_ethiopia_bot, type /browse or /search with a category and location. See verified listings with photos, specs, and daily rates in ETB.

🔒
2

Book & send deposit

Select your dates and use /book. Send a 20% deposit via Telebirr to secure your reservation. Our team verifies the payment within hours.

✅
3

Get confirmed & start

Once the owner confirms, you receive their contact details directly. Settle the remaining balance on pickup and get your project moving.
